# AI digit recognition P6: fixed device, Odin III simulation, datasheets (schema version 10)

Purpose: the AI design-pattern study (Peter 2026-11-10/11). Measures: area, speed, power and energy of every design
on its own auto-sized grid and on one fixed device; accuracy; whether Odin III can simulate the design.

## 1. Fixed-device comparison (target `vtr-k6-fixed`)

`aidigit_result` gets a third VTR-style target, `vtr-k6-fixed`, same columns as `vtr-k6`. Source:
`suites/ai-digit-recognition/results/fixed-vtr.jsonl`, written by `tools/fixed_device.py`.

- One grid and one channel width for every design. The grid is the largest auto-sized grid of the table rounded
  up to a multiple of ten; W is 1.3 x the largest minimum channel width, rounded up to a multiple of ten. The
  numbers are in the heading of the table section and in every row (`area_grid`, `power_channel_width`).
- Each design's existing flow BLIF is placed and routed again (`vpr --pack --place --route --analysis
  --device auto --device_width G --route_chan_width W --power`); no re-synthesis, so LUT and FF counts equal the
  auto-grid row. The stages are named explicitly because this VPR build otherwise runs the analytical placer,
  which the auto-grid rows did not use. Activity is ACE2 vectorless (-s 1), as before.
- `area_min_chan_width` is NULL with the reason "not searched". Latency and energy use the design's own cycles
  per image (same RTL) times the fixed-grid critical path. Static power is that of the whole grid.
- `clb_util_pct` is CLBs used over the CLB tiles of the fixed grid (`mimir.aidigit.clb_tiles`).

## 2. Odin III simulation (schema 10 columns)

Odin III v1.6.1 simulates `$mem` without bit-blasting: `read_verilog; proc; opt; lower --keep-mem-cells; sim_tb`.
`tools/odin_sim.py` runs the 100-image quick tier per design and writes `results/odin-sim.jsonl` (rows with an
`odin_sim` key, no `target`). Columns on every target row of the design: `odin_sim_status` (pass: class and score
match on all images; mismatch; error), `odin_sim_n`, `odin_sim_class_match`, `odin_sim_score_match`,
`odin_sim_cycles_per_s` (testbench cycles over wall time), `odin_sim_wall_s`, `odin_sim_build`. An error or a
design not run has NULL numbers and the cause in `aidigit_null` (the located Odin error line, or the time limit).

## 3. Other changes

- `tools[vtr_commit]` in `implementations.toml` (3c9a4d23b2, from the VPR version line of the run logs) fills
  `vtr_commit` on VTR rows; the NULL reason is gone.
- `score/vtr_leg.py` power step: the memory cap is 2 x the route step's largest `max_rss`, 2 G to 8 G (was a fixed
  2 G that killed VPR on bnn-M-f2); `--power-mem` overrides.
- `mimir migrate` accepts `--db STORE` as well as the positional.
- Datasheets: `mimir aidigit-table` also writes `datasheets/<design>.md` (`mimir/aidigitsheet.py`), from the store
  rows only.

Migration 9 -> 10 is additive (seven nullable columns). `merge --runs` needs version 10 on both sides.
